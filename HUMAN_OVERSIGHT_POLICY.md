# LIVE SYNESIS Human Oversight and Workforce Protection Policy

## Purpose

LIVE SYNESIS is a decision-support and decision-control system. It assists qualified people by extracting evidence, identifying risks, comparing alternatives, drafting options, mapping obligations and preparing review records. It is not an autonomous decision-maker and must not be represented as a replacement for accountable professionals.

## Human accountability rule

Every consequential outcome requires a named authorised human reviewer. The reviewer must verify the source evidence, consider relevant context, record a substantive rationale and accept personal or institutional accountability under the applicable authority matrix.

No analysis, recommendation, simulation, score or generated draft constitutes final approval by itself.

## Employment and workforce decisions

LIVE SYNESIS must not autonomously make, execute or communicate decisions concerning:

- hiring or candidate rejection;
- termination, dismissal or redundancy;
- promotion or demotion;
- compensation, incentives or benefits;
- disciplinary action;
- performance ratings, ranking or succession;
- access to livelihood, work assignments or professional opportunity.

For employment-related material, Synesis may organise evidence, identify policy or legal questions, detect inconsistencies, draft neutral review questions and show alternative interpretations. The final judgment must be made by authorised people using applicable law, policy, consultation and procedural fairness.

## Analysis lifecycle

1. **Evidence received** — the document or dataset remains the source of truth.
2. **AI analysis generated** — the result is advisory and carries provenance.
3. **Pending Human Review** — a qualified reviewer verifies evidence, assumptions and limitations.
4. **Controlled decision recorded** — the reviewer records rationale, owner, approval authority and completion evidence.
5. **Final status** — only an authorised role may close the matter after the required evidence exists.

## Provider and fallback rule

A deterministic fallback, provider error, exhausted quota, invalid key, unavailable model or incomplete model response must never be labelled as completed live analysis. The platform must return an explicit failure state, suppress completion, preserve the source safely and require reanalysis after service restoration.

## Audit requirements

The system must retain:

- the named reviewer and role;
- the decision timestamp;
- the reviewer’s rationale;
- the source document identity and analysis provenance;
- the approval status and completion evidence;
- provider or fallback failures relevant to the decision record.

## Permitted use

Synesis may assist Legal, Compliance, Risk, Operations, Investment, Governance and other authorised teams with research, review, comparison, drafting, control mapping and audit preparation. Users remain responsible for professional verification and final action.

## Product language

Use **assist**, **analyse**, **flag**, **compare**, **draft**, **recommend**, **review** and **record**. Do not describe Synesis as autonomously deciding, approving, hiring, firing, replacing staff or executing consequential actions.
